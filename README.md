# Best Online Shopping in Nepal

**[Neshop](https://neshop.com.np/)** is an online shopping marketplace in Nepal where customers can discover and shop for products from different sellers. Built to offer convenience and variety, Neshop connects shoppers directly with local merchants across Nepal in a unified digital marketplace.

## Neshop Features

Key features of the Neshop marketplace include:

- Online shopping from multiple product categories
- Products from local sellers and suppliers
- Convenient product discovery and ordering
- Seller-focused marketplace ecosystem
- Availability across Nepal
- Simple and user-friendly shopping experience

## Neshop Seller Portal

Neshop provides a dedicated seller and supplier platform for businesses and individual merchants who want to sell products online. A key benefit of the platform is **0% commission for sellers**, allowing merchants to use the **[Neshop Seller Portal](https://neshop.com.np/supplier)** to manage their products and participate in the marketplace without standard commission fees.

## Neshop Partner Program

The **[Neshop Partner Program](https://neshop.com.np/partner)** provides an opportunity for partners, creators, and promoters to share Neshop with their audience and earn through the program.
